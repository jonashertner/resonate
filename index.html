how

what this is, what it keeps, what it shares.

what this is

An atlas of places that matter to you. It lives in your browser. There is no account, no server, no tracking. Close the tab and it waits. Erase it and it is gone.

what it keeps

Your places, tags, notes, stars, photos. All of it stays on your device. Photos never leave it. Nothing is collected. Nobody can read your atlas.

what it shares

Nothing, until you hand someone a link. A link carries only what you put in it: the places of a folio, or your atlas without photos. An ask carries one question and your name. That is all.

how recommendation works

You hand a folio to one person. A titled, signed set of places. When someone hands you theirs, your device compares the two atlases: what you both hold, what you both love, where they know ground you do not. The verdict is a word, never a score.

Keep a person as a voice and their marks stay on your field, open rings you may adopt. An adopted place says after their name, forever.

the newsstand

Anyone may publish a folio to the commons. Your device fetches the list and ranks it against your own atlas. The ranking happens on your device. The newsstand never learns what you like, what you read, or who you are.
